## EEAT Content Rules

Every article should include:

- Author line: `By FlashPet Editorial Team`
- Review line: `Reviewed for product accuracy and pet-home safety by the FlashPet product team`
- Last updated date.
- A real pet-home scenario in the introduction.
- A practical checklist or decision table.
- FAQ section with 4 to 6 questions.
- References section for health, safety, or air-cleaner claims.
- Clear product limitation language.

Required claim language:

- Use: `helps reduce`, `helps neutralize`, `supports fresher indoor air`, `when used as directed`.
- Avoid: `kills all bacteria`, `removes all allergens`, `guarantees odor removal`, `medical-grade`, `cures allergies`.
- For ionizer and ozone topics, state clearly that ozone generators are different from low-ozone certified air cleaners and should not be used casually around people or pets.
